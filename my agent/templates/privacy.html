Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2024

1. Introduction

Nova Brief ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") operates the Nova Brief website and newsletter service. This Privacy Policy explains our online information practices and the choices you can make about how your information is used.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in the following ways:

  • Email Address: When you subscribe to our newsletter
  • Name: When you voluntarily provide it during registration
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our website (pages visited, time spent, etc.)
  • Device Information: Browser type, IP address, and device type
  • Contact Information: When you submit the contact form

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for various purposes:

  • To send you our daily news briefing
  • To communicate with you about your subscription
  • To respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • To improve our website and services
  • To analyze website usage and trends
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Email Communications

When you subscribe to Nova Brief, you agree to receive our daily news briefing via email. You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting us directly.

6. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services (such as email providers and analytics platforms) to support our operations. These services may have access to your information as necessary to perform their functions, but they are contractually obligated to use it only for the purposes we specify.

7. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance your experience. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, but this may affect functionality of our website.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to access your data
  • Right to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to delete your data
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at supportnovabrief@gmail.com
