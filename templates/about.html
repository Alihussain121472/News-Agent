Our Mission & Background

Empowering Tech Students with Real Opportunities

Nova Brief helps tech students in Pakistan and South Asia. Find internships, grants, and useful tech news in one place.

Who We Are & Why This Exists

Nova Brief started in 2026 as a student project. It was built by Syed Ali Hussain, an AI college student in Pakistan.

Like many tech and AI students, Ali found it hard to track grants, internships, and courses across many sites. Often, students hear about a program only after it has closed.

Nova Brief puts these programs and tech news in one place. Each post links to the official page so you can check open dates and rules before you apply.

What We Track

Internships & Fellowships

Links to open source programs (GSoC, LFX, MLH) and global tech internships. Check each organizer’s page for the current deadlines and country requirements.

Scholarships & Grants

Find college and grad school grants like Erasmus Mundus, HEC programs, and global fellowships. Each listing notes who can apply.

Free Tech Certifications & Credits

Find real, free tech certificates, cloud credits (AWS, Azure, GCP), and GitHub student perks. There are no fake paywalls.

Technology Briefing

Read short updates on software and AI news. We link to the original source when one is available.

Editorial & Listing Standards

We know students waste hours applying to expired or scam listings. We adhere to four strict principles:

  • Organizer Links: Each post links to the host or source. Check the host page and rules before you apply.
  • Eligibility: Rules can change for each round. Check the host page for country, student, and age rules.
  • Dates: We show a deadline when one is available, but dates can change. Treat the official organizer page as the current source of truth.
  • Clear Partnerships: We do not make up reader counts or reviews. We label paid posts and partner links clearly. Money never changes our reviews or program rules.
