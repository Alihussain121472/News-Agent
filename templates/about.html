Our Mission & Background

Empowering Tech Students with Real Opportunities

Nova Brief is a deadline first opportunity and learning tracker built for Pakistani and South Asian tech students to find verified internships, scholarships, and practical tech news.

Who We Are & Why This Exists

Founded in 2026, Nova Brief is an independent student project created by Syed Ali Hussain, a BS Artificial Intelligence student in Pakistan.

Like thousands of computer science and AI students across South Asia, Ali faced a constant frustration: valuable opportunities such as Google Summer of Code, global remote internships, university scholarships, and free certification programs are scattered across dozens of corporate portals, blogs, and buried LinkedIn posts. By the time students hear about them in university WhatsApp groups, the deadline has often already passed.

Nova Brief was built to fix that. We track official deadlines, verify eligibility specifically for Pakistani and South Asian applicants, and curate a noise free daily briefing so students have enough runway to prepare strong applications.

What We Track

Verified Internships & Fellowships

Tracked deadlines, country eligibility checks, and direct application links for open source programs (GSoC, LFX, MLH) and global tech internships.

Scholarships & Grants

Undergraduate and graduate scholarship opportunities (Erasmus Mundus, HEC initiatives, international fellowships) with eligibility criteria.

Free Tech Certifications & Credits

Legitimate, zero cost certifications, cloud vouchers (AWS, Azure, GCP), and GitHub student benefits without fake paywalls.

5 to 8 Story Daily Tech Briefing

A clean, noise free summary of real engineering developments and AI breakthroughs, skipping package releases and promotional fluff.

Trust & Verification Rules

We know students waste hours applying to expired or scam listings. We adhere to four strict principles:

  • Official Sources Only: Every opportunity links directly to the official company portal, university site, or program foundation. No third party ad redirect traps.
  • Eligibility First: We clearly specify whether Pakistani and international students are eligible so you don't waste time preparing ineligible applications.
  • Verified Dates: Each listing notes the deadline and when the opportunity was last verified.
  • Zero Fake Metrics: We do not fabricate subscriber counts, fake student reviews, or exaggerated partnerships. Any sponsored or affiliate links in the future will be clearly and explicitly disclosed.
