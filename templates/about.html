Our Mission & Background

Empowering Tech Students with Real Opportunities

Nova Brief is an opportunity and learning tracker built for Pakistani and South Asian tech students to find internships, scholarships, and practical tech news.

Who We Are & Why This Exists

Founded in 2026, Nova Brief is an independent student project created by Syed Ali Hussain, a BS Artificial Intelligence student in Pakistan.

Like many computer science and AI students, Ali found it difficult to keep track of internships, scholarships, and learning programs spread across company portals and community posts. Sometimes students hear about an opportunity after its application window has closed.

Nova Brief brings these opportunities and technology news into one place. We link to organizer pages so students can check the current application window and eligibility before applying.

What We Track

Internships & Fellowships

Links to open source programs (GSoC, LFX, MLH) and global tech internships. Check each organizer’s page for the current deadlines and country requirements.

Scholarships & Grants

Undergraduate and graduate scholarship opportunities (Erasmus Mundus, HEC initiatives, international fellowships) with eligibility criteria.

Free Tech Certifications & Credits

Legitimate, zero cost certifications, cloud vouchers (AWS, Azure, GCP), and GitHub student benefits without fake paywalls.

Technology Briefing

Concise summaries of engineering and AI developments, with links to original sources where available.

Editorial & Listing Standards

We know students waste hours applying to expired or scam listings. We adhere to four strict principles:

  • Organizer Links: Listings include a link to the organizer or a source for the opportunity. Confirm the current application page and requirements before applying.
  • Eligibility: Requirements can change by program and application cycle. Check the organizer’s page for current country, student-status, and age criteria.
  • Dates: We show a deadline when one is available, but dates can change. Treat the official organizer page as the current source of truth.
  • Clear Partnerships: We do not invent audience numbers or testimonials. Paid placements and affiliate links are identified near the relevant content; payment does not determine editorial conclusions or program eligibility.
