Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit our website. They help us remember information about your visit, such as your preferred theme (dark or light mode) and other settings. This makes your next visit to our website easier and the website more useful to you.

2. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for core site security, CSRF protection, and user login sessions.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your theme preference (dark/light mode) and site settings.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us anonymously analyze aggregate page view metrics and visitor trends.
  • Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense): Used by Google and third-party advertising partners to deliver relevant advertisements and measure ad performance.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

First-Party Cookies

These are cookies set by Nova Brief directly. They maintain your authentication session and interface preferences.

Third-Party & Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense)

We partner with Google AdSense to display relevant advertising across our public editorial pages. Google uses cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on prior visits to our site and other websites across the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or AboutAds.info.

Session vs. Persistent Cookies

Session cookies expire when you close your browser, while persistent cookies remain on your device until they reach their expiration date or you manually clear them.

4. Managing and Disabling Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies at any time through your browser preferences (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). Please note that disabling essential cookies may prevent you from logging into your account.

5. Policy Revisions

We may periodically update this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in technical standards or legal requirements.
